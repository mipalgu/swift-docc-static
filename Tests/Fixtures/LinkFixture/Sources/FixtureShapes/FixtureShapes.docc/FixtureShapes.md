# ``FixtureShapes``

Swatches and paints that use the colours of another package.

## Overview

A ``Swatch`` shows a ``FixtureColours/Colour``, and every ``Paint`` is a
``FixtureColours/Pigment``.  Read <doc:MixingPaints> to mix paints, starting with
<doc:MixingPaints#Choosing-Pigments>.
