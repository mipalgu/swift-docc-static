# Mixing Paints

Combine pigments into paints.

## Overview

Every ``Paint`` is a ``FixtureColours/Pigment``, and its ``Paint/swatch``
shows a ``FixtureColours/Colour`` that conforms to ``FixtureColours/Tinted``.

### Choosing Pigments

Pick a pigment for each ``Swatch``.
